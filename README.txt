

Situation: Your research team has arrived at the Worldwide Observatory for Research on Macroparasite Stuff (WORMS), where you're excited to begin your summer research internship. However, even before you first day orientation things start going a bit wriggly around the WORMS lab. Help the research team keep their helminth survellience systems running by solving a series of coding problems!

These are materials created for a University of South Carolina CodeFest, developed and delivered through the support of NSF Grant ##################. Delivered in R, these problems should hopefully
- help beginner/intermediate coders develop R skills, including indexing, loops, etc
- help even advanced R users think about data formats and basic coding practices outside of their everyday coding toolbox. 

These problems are adapted from select challenges in Google's [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/), an annual series of daily coding challenges Google has published each December since 2015. They've been conceptually redisgned to fit a helminth theme, but otherwise have been changed very little! 

All of these problems can be solved multiple ways! I envision these challenges being presented to teams or small groups, give the groups time to work, and then share back their approaches and solutions to the larger group.
